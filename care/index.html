Care

Care built around the whole picture.

Four services, offered by nurse practitioners and clinical social workers who develop a plan with you rather than for you.

60–90 minutes

Initial psychiatric evaluation

We get to know you and what is prompting a change. We ask a lot of questions so we are looking at the full picture, then talk through your options together: supplements, therapy, further evaluations and medication.

15–30 minutes

Follow-up visits

Shorter check-ins to review how you are doing and how you are responding to treatment, covering supplements, medication, lifestyle adjustments and your physical health.

Reviewed at a follow-up

Genetic testing

Testing that shows how your body metabolizes medications, and whether you carry the MTHFR gene mutation. We go through the results with you, including what they suggest about how you may respond to specific medications.

Tailored to you

Individual therapy

Space to explore how your emotions, thoughts and behaviors connect with your life now, using approaches chosen for your needs and aimed at long-term gains. We do not specialize in Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT).

Common questions

Good to know before you come

How long is the first appointment?

Plan for 60 to 90 minutes. We use the time to get to know you and to look at the full picture before talking through treatment options.

What happens at follow-up visits?

Follow-ups are 15 to 30 minute check-ins. We review your progress and how you are responding to treatment, including supplements, medication, lifestyle adjustments and your physical health.

Do you only prescribe medication?

No. At the end of your first visit we talk through a range of options, which can include supplements, therapy, additional evaluations and medication.

What is the genetic testing for?

It shows how your body metabolizes medications and whether you carry the MTHFR gene mutation. We review the results with you at a follow-up visit, including what they suggest about how you may respond to particular medications.

Do you offer DBT?

We offer individual therapy with approaches tailored to each person, but we do not specialize in Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT). Our resource directory lists local therapists we refer to.

How soon can I be seen?

We are currently scheduling within 3 weeks. You can see open times and book online.

Is this the right place in an emergency?

No. If you need immediate help, call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline. Our crisis page lists more national and local lines.
