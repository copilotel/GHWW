Care

What we offer

All about our care →
60–90 minutes

Initial psychiatric evaluation

We get to know you and what is prompting a change. We ask a lot of questions so we are looking at the full picture, then talk through your options together: supplements, therapy, further evaluations and medication.

How it works →
15–30 minutes

Follow-up visits

Shorter check-ins to review how you are doing and how you are responding to treatment, covering supplements, medication, lifestyle adjustments and your physical health.

How it works →
Reviewed at a follow-up

Genetic testing

Testing that shows how your body metabolizes medications, and whether you carry the MTHFR gene mutation. We go through the results with you, including what they suggest about how you may respond to specific medications.

How it works →
Tailored to you

Individual therapy

Space to explore how your emotions, thoughts and behaviors connect with your life now, using approaches chosen for your needs and aimed at long-term gains. We do not specialize in Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT).

How it works →
This isn’t something we do for you. It’s something we do with you.

How we work

Our first priority when we meet is that you feel safe, comfortable and heard, and that you stay in charge of what happens next.

Everyone’s mental health needs are different. Your treatment plan is built around who you are and what you want for yourself, by people who support you wholeheartedly.
